The difference

Most of this job happens before the first paver goes down.

Anyone can lay stone on sand. It looks the same on day one — and it sinks by year three. We dig the base out, compact it properly and check the depth before anything gets laid.

That's the part nobody photographs and the only part that decides whether your patio still sits flat in ten years. It's slower, it costs more, and we don't skip it.
