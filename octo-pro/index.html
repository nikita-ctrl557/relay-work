The batch

Annotated before / after

Your own Eastvale job, marked up by hand.

The red pen is the point. A clean before/after gets scrolled past because every coating company posts them. An annotation forces the eye to stop and read one specific claim, and the claim answers the real objection: will the cracks come back.

Where it runs: Cold traffic. This is the one to spend the most budget on.

Nine-city proof grid

Nine real jobs, each labelled with the town it was in.

Naming the town is the highest-performing device in local trades, because it does proof and geo-targeting in the same square inch. Someone in Tustin sees Irvine and Anaheim and reads you as their guy, not a national franchise.

Where it runs: Warm traffic and retargeting. Also your best organic post.

Epoxy vs polyaspartic

The argument your current ad already makes, but buried.

You are the only advertiser in your market with a real technical reason to be chosen, and it is sitting in paragraph six. Pulled to the top it becomes a filter: it disqualifies price shoppers and pre-sells anyone who was about to buy epoxy.

Where it runs: Mid-funnel. Feed it people who watched 50% of a video.

Honest scarcity

Real dates, and a reason the calendar is finite.

This replaces "3 Floors left in August", which has been live since 31 January. Scarcity only works when it is falsifiable. Naming actual weeks and giving the reason (one crew, one floor a day) makes the limit believable instead of decorative.

Where it runs: Bottom of funnel. Rotate the dates every fortnight.
